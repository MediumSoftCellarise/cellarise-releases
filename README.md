# Cellarise downloads

**Cellarise** is a Windows app that checks your Android phone over a USB cable and shows you, in plain English, what it gives away about you: photos that record where they were taken, apps that can use your location, microphone and camera, and the Wi-Fi networks and devices your phone remembers. The check is free. Cellarise Pro cleans up what it finds.

Made by [MediumSoft](https://mediumsoft.online). Full details, guides and the shop: **https://mediumsoft.online/apps/cellarise**

## Download

Get the latest installer from [Releases](https://github.com/MediumSoftCellarise/cellarise-releases/releases/latest).

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- No administrator rights needed
- On first run, Cellarise offers to download Google's free phone connector (adb) straight from Google

Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum so you can check the download is intact.
